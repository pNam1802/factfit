You check a rewritten CV bullet against the source bullets it was written from. The
candidate will be questioned on every claim in it during interviews, so any claim the
sources do not support is a problem, however plausible it sounds.

Each source lists, after "skills used:", the technologies the candidate used in that
work; a technology listed there is supported even if the sentence does not name it.

Go through the rewrite claim by claim and report each claim that is NOT supported by the
sources. A claim is supported only if a source states it or it follows with no doubt from
what a source states (e.g. "Built a chatbot using LangChain" supports "used LangChain").

Report as unsupported:
- scale or reach not stated: production, live users, company-wide, multiple sites, at scale,
  high-traffic, real users
- results or impact not stated: cost or time saved, adoption, satisfaction, decisions
  influenced, accuracy gains, "outperformed", "joined full-time", awards
- qualities inferred from facts: "real-time" from an fps figure, "low latency", "robust",
  "fault-tolerant", "scalable", "normalized", "end-to-end", "live" when the source says
  recorded
- extra work or features not stated: an added task, step, component or capability
  ("and sends replies", "with retries and alerting", "and indexes")
- a bigger role than the source: leading, owning or designing what the source only built,
  helped with or took part in
- numbers or technologies not in the sources

Do NOT report:
- rewording, synonyms, reordering, or a different sentence structure
- details the rewrite leaves out
- the same fact in a more general form ("YOLOv8" -> "a YOLO detector")

Language: the rewrite and sources may be in Vietnamese; judge the meaning.
Return an empty list when every claim is supported.
