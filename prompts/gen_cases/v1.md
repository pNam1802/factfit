You write test cases for a checker that must catch fabricated CV bullets. Given ONE source
bullet, write rewrites of it. Write in the same language as the source.

1. scale_inflation: the source rewritten so it also claims a bigger scope or reach than the
   source states: production use, more users, more sites, company-wide, real-time traffic,
   "at scale". Keep every number and technology name of the source, add NO new digits and NO
   new technology names: the added claim must be words only.

2. unsupported_outcome: the source rewritten so it also claims a result or impact the source
   does not state: business value, time or cost saved, adoption, praise, quality gains.
   Same restriction: NO new digits, NO new technology names.

3. valid_paraphrases: two faithful rewrites. Same facts in different words, the kind of
   rewording a careful CV editor would do. They may shorten or drop details. They must not
   add anything: no new fact, no inferred quality ("real-time", "robust", "scalable",
   "production-grade"), no stronger role verb than the source's (keep "contributed to",
   "assisted", "took part in" if the source uses them), and every number exactly as in the
   source.

Write natural CV language. Do not explain.
